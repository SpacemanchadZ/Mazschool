    Lesson guide

    Tens and ones at the Moon Bakery. About 45 minutes for ages 7–8. Check things off as you go; your progress is saved on this device.

    0 of 6 steps done

    What they’ll learn

    1. Count by 10s to 100 and back down.
    2. Build any number from 11 to 99 as tens and ones, and say it both ways (47 is 4 tens and 7 ones).
    3. Trade 10 ones for 1 ten.
    4. Add two two-digit numbers by adding the tens, then the ones.
    5. Tell which of two numbers is bigger by looking at the tens first.

    Get ready

    Gather these before the kids sit down so the lesson keeps moving.

    Words to use

    Tap a card to see the bakery version. Have the kids say each word back.

    Teach it step by step

    Two kids, one game

    1. The first kid types their name on the Play screen and plays all four stations.
    2. The other kid builds each problem with real cartons and counters at the same time.
    3. When the first kid finishes, tap Next baker’s turn (tap twice to confirm). Their stars are saved to the bakers list.
    4. Switch jobs and play again. Every turn gets new problems.

    Make it easier or harder

    Check-in
